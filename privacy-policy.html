Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 16, 2026

Ashfires Press operates internal analytics tools used to understand the performance of our websites, publications, and content.

Google and YouTube Data

Our internal analytics application may connect to Google services, including YouTube, through Google APIs. Access occurs only after authorization by an authorized Ashfires account.

The application uses authorized data solely to collect and store analytics information for Ashfires-owned or managed properties. This may include YouTube channel, video, traffic, engagement, and performance analytics.

We do not use this access to collect analytics for unrelated third parties.

How We Use Data

Analytics data is used internally to measure content performance, understand audience engagement, maintain historical metrics, and support publishing and business decisions.

Data Sharing

We do not sell Google or YouTube user data.

Data obtained through Google APIs is used for Ashfires' internal analytics purposes and is not shared with third parties except when necessary to operate our infrastructure or when required by law.

Google API Services

Our use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will comply with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including applicable Limited Use requirements.

Revoking Access

Authorized users may revoke the application's access through their Google Account security settings. Revoking access prevents the application from obtaining new authorized Google or YouTube data.

Contact

Questions about this policy may be directed to Ashfires Press through the contact information provided on our website.

Ashfires is an independent creative and research ecosystem.
